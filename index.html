How to read the pronunciation columnguía

the vowels are the same as in Spanish (a, e, i, o, u).

  • r is a single tap, like the r in "pero". Never roll it.
  • h is a soft English h, softer than the Spanish j.
  • j is the English j in "jeans".
  • z is a buzzing English z. Don't turn it into an s.
  • A doubled vowel (oo, aa, ii) means hold that vowel twice as long. The length changes the meaning.
  • Endings in -des / -mas: the final u is silent.
  • Stress: keep it flat. Japanese has no strong stressed syllable.
